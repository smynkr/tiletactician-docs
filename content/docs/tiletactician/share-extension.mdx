## What it does

The iOS Share Extension passes a shared screenshot into TileTactician's
authenticated scan flow. The app reads the board, opens the editor with
recognized tiles, and still requires you to verify the board, enter the rack,
and start analysis. Sharing does not bypass scan credits or make rack entry
automatic.

## How to use

1. Take a screenshot of the board (or save a board image).

2. Tap the **Share** button in an iOS app and choose **TileTactician** from the
   share sheet.

3. The extension passes the image to TileTactician, which reads the board and
   opens the editor with recognized tiles prefilled.

4. Verify or correct the recognized board cells and enter your rack manually.

5. Tap **Analyze Board** to run the solver.

## Enabling the extension

If TileTactician does not appear in the share sheet:

1. Open any app and tap **Share**.

2. Scroll the share sheet all the way to the right and tap **More** (`···`).

3. Find **TileTactician** in the list and toggle it on.

4. Optionally drag it to the top of the list for quick access.

## Accuracy

The extension uses image recognition to read tiles from the screenshot.
Accuracy depends on:

- **Image clarity** — a clean, well-lit screenshot works best

- **Board alignment** — the board should fill most of the frame

- **App rendering** — screenshots from the NYT Games app and official Scrabble
  apps are well-supported

Always verify the imported board before analyzing. Misread tiles produce
incorrect results. See [Board entry](/tiletactician/board-entry) for how to correct
individual cells.

## Limitations

- iOS only (the Share Extension is an iOS platform feature)

- One board per share — sharing multiple images processes only the first

- The extension does not read the rack; enter your tiles manually after import
