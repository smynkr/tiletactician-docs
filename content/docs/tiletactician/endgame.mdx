When the bag is empty, TileTactician solves the endgame — exact opponent tiles, optimal sequence, and win probability.

## What is endgame mode?

When the tile bag is empty, both players' tiles are fully determined: your rack
is visible, and your opponent's rack is everything not on the board, not on
your rack, and not in the (empty) bag. TileTactician's endgame solver uses this
perfect information to find the optimal play sequence.

## Activating endgame

Endgame mode activates automatically when:

- The tile bag count reaches zero (tracked via [Tile bag](/tiletactician/tile-bag))

- You manually toggle it from the board toolbar (endgame icon)

## What the solver computes

The endgame panel shows:

- **Opponent's rack** — the exact tiles your opponent holds (deduced from
  perfect information)

- **Optimal sequence** — the play order that maximizes your final score margin,
  assuming optimal play from both sides

- **Win/loss projection** — whether the current position is a win or loss under
  perfect play, and the projected point margin

- **Key decisions** — critical junctures where the choice of play changes the
  outcome

## Reading the endgame panel

| Field                | Meaning                                            |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your rack**        | Tiles you currently hold                           |
| **Opponent rack**    | Deduced opponent tiles                             |
| **Best play**        | The solver's recommended move                      |
| **Projected margin** | Expected final score difference (+you / −opponent) |
| **Depth**            | How many turns ahead the solver has searched       |

## Performance

Endgame solving is computationally intensive — it searches the full game tree
of remaining turns. Typical solve times:

| Tiles remaining  | Time (Apple Silicon) |
| ---------------- | -------------------- |
| ≤ 4 tiles total  | \<  1 second         |
| 5–8 tiles total  | 2–10 seconds         |
| 9–14 tiles total | 10–60 seconds        |

The solver runs incrementally and displays the best line found so far while
searching deeper.

## Limitations

- Endgame mode assumes both players play optimally. A human opponent may not
  follow the projected sequence.

- The solver uses the embedded dictionary; if your game uses a different word
  list, results may differ.

- With more than 14 tiles remaining, the search space grows exponentially and
  the solver may not reach full depth.
