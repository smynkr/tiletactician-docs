## Tile bag

The tile bag tracker starts from the standard 100-tile English Scrabble
distribution and subtracts tiles that are already on the board or in your
rack:

| Letter | Count | Letter | Count | Letter | Count |
| ------ | ----: | ------ | ----: | ------ | ----: |
| A      |     9 | J      |     1 | S      |     4 |
| B      |     2 | K      |     1 | T      |     6 |
| C      |     2 | L      |     4 | U      |     4 |
| D      |     4 | M      |     2 | V      |     2 |
| E      |    12 | N      |     6 | W      |     2 |
| F      |     2 | O      |     8 | X      |     1 |
| G      |     3 | P      |     2 | Y      |     2 |
| H      |     2 | Q      |     1 | Z      |     1 |
| I      |     9 | R      |     6 | Blank  |     2 |

The summary card shows the total number of tiles remaining. It also calls out
remaining high-value tiles (`J`, `Q`, `X`, and `Z`) when any are still in the
bag.

## How tracking works

TileTactician tracks three pools:

1. **On the board** — tiles placed during the game.
2. **On your rack** — your current tiles.
3. **In the bag** — the distribution total minus the first two pools.

As you update the board or rack, the per-letter count and total adjust
automatically. Blank tiles are counted as blanks rather than as the letter they
represent.

## Next-seven-draw probability

Each remaining letter displays a **per-letter draw probability**: the chance of
drawing at least one copy of that letter in the next seven draws (or in all
remaining draws when fewer than seven tiles remain). It is not a bingo
probability and it does not claim that the seven drawn tiles form a word.

The display uses the exact without-replacement calculation for the probability
of drawing zero copies, then subtracts that value from one. In compact form:

`P(at least one) = 1 - P(none in the next 7 draws)`

A letter with no remaining copies shows 0%; a full bag containing only that
letter would show 100%. The percentage is recalculated from the current board,
rack, and total bag count.

## Using tile tracking strategically

- **Watch the endgame threshold.** When the unseen total is seven tiles or
  fewer, [Endgame analysis](/tiletactician/endgame) can infer the opponent's
  rack from the remaining distribution.
- **Compare exchange candidates.** The percentages tell you how likely each
  letter is to appear over a seven-draw window, rather than only on the very
  next draw.
- **Track high-value tiles.** The summary callout makes it easy to see whether
  `J`, `Q`, `X`, or `Z` remains available.

## Crossplay differences

The app uses the same standard 100-tile distribution for its Scrabble and
Crossplay tracking. Switching board type changes the board layout, but the tile
bag counts and per-letter draw calculation use this distribution in both
formats.
