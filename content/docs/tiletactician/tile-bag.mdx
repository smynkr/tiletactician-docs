## How tracking works

TileTactician tracks tiles across three pools:

1. **On the board** — tiles placed during the game

2. **On your rack** — your current tiles

3. **In the bag** — everything else (calculated by subtraction)

As you update the board and rack, the bag count adjusts automatically. The
tracker shows:

- **Remaining count** per letter

- **Draw probability** — the chance of drawing a specific letter on the next
  draw

- **Bingo probability** — the chance of drawing 7 tiles that form at least one
  bingo-eligible combination

## Using tile tracking strategically

- **Endgame** — when the bag is empty, you know exactly what your opponent
  holds. See [Endgame analysis](/tiletactician/endgame).

- **Exchange decisions** — if the bag is rich in vowels, exchanging consonants
  is more likely to improve your rack.

- **Defensive play** — if high-value tiles (Q, Z, X) are still in the bag,
  avoid opening premium squares your opponent could exploit.

## Crossplay differences

NYT Crossplay uses the same tile distribution as Scrabble. If a future Crossplay
variant changes the distribution, update it in Settings → Tile Distribution.
