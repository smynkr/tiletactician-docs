title: Study mode and saved words
description: Turn saved plays into a mastery deck, practice two- and
  three-letter words, and manage your personal word bank.
---

**Study** turns the embedded word list and your saved plays into short,
repeatable flashcard sessions. Open **Word Study** from the app, then choose a
category before starting a session.

## Choose a study category

The category screen offers:

- **Your Word Bank** — the words you saved from analysis results;
- **2-Letter Words** — every two-letter word in the loaded trie; and
- **3-Letter Words** — every three-letter word in the loaded trie.

The app loads its dictionary before it builds the two- and three-letter decks.
The Word Bank option is disabled until at least one word is saved. Its progress
line shows how many saved words are familiar and allows up to 10 cards per
session. The two- and three-letter sessions allow up to 20 cards.

## How a flashcard session works

Each card shows the word as tiles, its tile value, and (for a Word Bank card)
its saved context when available: `Crossplay` or `Scrabble`, the score, and the
saved board position.

Choose one answer for every card:

- **Familiar** records a correct answer and advances to the next card.
- **Keep Practicing** records an attempt without adding a correct answer.

The app saves each answer to device storage as it is recorded. A word counts as
mastered/familiar after two correct answers. Decks prioritize words with fewer
correct answers, then fewer attempts; the Word Bank also uses save recency and
alphabetical order as tie-breakers. If you end a session early, the answers
already recorded remain saved on that device.

At the end, the summary reports how many cards you marked familiar and how many
words in the selected category currently meet the familiarity threshold. Choose
**Study Again** to repeat the category or **Choose Category** to return to the
selection screen.

## Build and manage your Word Bank

From any result row, use the bookmark/save control to add a word. The **My
Words** screen lets you:

- view saved words and their score/position metadata;
- search the list when it contains more than five entries;
- tap the definition control for a word lookup;
- remove a word with the `×` control; and
- open a personalized study session with **Practice Saved Words**.

The study deck deduplicates saved entries by uppercase word and keeps the most
recent saved record for that word. A word can retain its board type, score, and
position as context while you practice it.

The definition sheet displays the word's **definition**, optional **part of
speech**, and optional **phonetic** spelling. It does not promise an example
sentence or other fields. The lookup can show a loading state, a missing
definition, a timeout, or an unavailable response.

## Sync saved words

The My Words screen loads the local word bank first. When you are signed in, it
then pulls the account's cloud words and pushes unsynced local rows through the
same account-scoped sync flow. The status banner reads **Syncing…** during the
operation and **Synced across devices** when it finishes.

Cloud merges compare save timestamps; a strictly newer cloud save replaces an
older local row. Deletes are represented by tombstones, and a deletion wins an
equal timestamp so a stale cloud row cannot resurrect a removed word. Anonymous
and signed-in word rows remain separate account buckets.

For board snapshots and saved game states, see [Boards library](/tiletactician/boards-library).
