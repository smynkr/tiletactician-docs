## How it works

TileTactician can read a Scrabble or Crossplay board from a photo or
screenshot. Tap **Scan Board** on the home screen, and the app uses on-device
image recognition to identify tiles, premium squares, and grid alignment.

Each board scan consumes one Vision scan credit. Your account includes 50
credits each month, and you can add more from Settings → Crossplay Pro; the
app shows your remaining balance before scanning.

## Scanning from the app

1. On the home screen, tap **Scan Board**.

2. Point your camera at a physical board, or choose a screenshot from your
   photo library.

3. The scanner highlights detected tiles and prompts you to review any
   uncertain reads.

4. Correct any misreads, then confirm the board to load it into the editor.

## Scanning from the Share Extension

On iOS, you can share a board screenshot from any app directly to
TileTactician. See [Share Extension](/tiletactician/share-extension) for setup.

## Accuracy tips

- **Flat, well-lit surface** — shadows and glare cause misreads

- **Fill the frame** — the board should occupy most of the image

- **Straight angle** — avoid extreme perspective; the scanner corrects mild
  skew but not heavy distortion

- **Physical boards** — matte tiles scan better than glossy ones

## Common misreads

| Misread   | Cause                        | Fix                                     |
| --------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| O ↔ empty | Round tile on a light square | Tap the cell and set the correct letter |
| I ↔ L ↔ 1 | Narrow tiles in some fonts   | Tap to correct                          |
| S ↔ 5     | Low-resolution images        | Retake at higher resolution             |
| Blank ↔ O | Unmarked blank tile          | Toggle the `?` blank marker on the cell |

## Scan reference overlay

After a scan, the **Show Scan** control in the editor toolbar overlays the
original scan image on top of the board grid, so you can verify the
recognized tiles against the photo before analyzing. Toggle it off with
**Hide Scan** to see the board cleanly.
