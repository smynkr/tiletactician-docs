## General

### Is TileTactician free?

The public browser solver is available at
[tiletactician.com/solver](https://tiletactician.com/solver/); check that page
for its current pricing and limits. The product repository also links to an
iOS App Store listing, but that URL returned 404 on 2026-10-09, so current iOS
installation availability is unverified. Once installed, native board analysis
runs locally and does not use scan credits. Board scans require sign-in and
Vision credits. Rack scans use a separate flow. See
[Settings](/tiletactician/settings-subscriptions) for the app's purchase
options.

### Does it work offline?

The mobile board solver and bundled word-list asset run locally after the app
is installed. Board scanning, account sync, and remote word definitions need a
network connection; the browser solver also requires internet access.

### What word list does it use?

The Settings screen labels the active list **NWL (NASPA)** and reports
**172,000+** words. The app repository does not identify the exact edition or
provenance of its bundled word-list asset, so that label does not establish a
specific current tournament-list version.

### Does it support Words With Friends?

Not currently. TileTactician supports Scrabble and NYT Crossplay board
formats. Words With Friends uses a different board layout and letter
distribution.

## Analysis

### Why are some plays missing from the results?

The native solver returns at most 100 unique placements, ordered by score and
then word. A legal play can be absent because it falls outside that cap; its
absence from the returned list does not prove that the play is illegal. For
possible board or rack-entry mistakes:

- **Check the board.** A missing or misplaced tile changes which words are
  formable. See [Board entry](/tiletactician/board-entry).

- **Check your rack.** Make sure the rack matches your actual tiles exactly,
  including blanks.

- **Check blanks.** A blank entered as a fixed letter is not a wildcard.

### Why is the score different from what I calculated?

Premium squares are the most common cause. Verify that:

- Tiles on premium squares are placed on the correct color

- Word premiums stack multiplicatively (double-word × double-word = 4×)

- Cross-word scores are included in the total

### The analysis is slow

The product README lists a sub-500 ms mid-game benchmark on Apple Silicon; that
is a development-machine benchmark, not a mobile-device performance guarantee.

- Close background apps to free memory.

- Ensure the board has no empty interior gaps (holes between tiles), which
  increase the search space.

- Restart the app if the solver appears stuck.

## Share Extension

### TileTactician does not appear in the share sheet

See [Share Extension → Enabling the extension](/tiletactician/share-extension#enabling-the-extension).

### The imported board has wrong tiles

Image recognition is not perfect. Always verify the board after import. Common
misreads:

- **O / 0** — the letter O vs. an empty cell

- **I / L / 1** — similar shapes in some app renderings

- **S / 5** — in low-resolution screenshots

Tap any cell to correct it before analyzing.
