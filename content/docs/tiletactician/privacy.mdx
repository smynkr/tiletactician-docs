## 1. Data we process

**Board scans.** When you use board scanning, the board photo is sent to our
server and processed by Google Vision (optical character recognition) and
OpenAI (board reconstruction). Images are processed and not stored — no API
keys ever ship on the device. Scan credits gate usage (your plan includes
monthly credits; purchased credits never expire).

**Subscriptions and sign-in.** Sign-in uses Apple. Billing, payment details,
and subscription management are handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit); payment
card details never touch our servers. We store only the entitlements needed to
honor your plan (scan credits, packs).

**Diagnostics and product analytics.** The app initializes two telemetry
services at launch:

- **Sentry** — crash, error, and performance diagnostics. An optional mobile
  replay is only enabled for specific native builds; it masks all text
  inputs and images and never captures logs or network contents.
- **PostHog** — anonymous product-usage analytics (screen/feature usage).
  Autocapture of screens is disabled. An optional session replay is only
  enabled for specific native builds; it masks all text inputs and images and
  never captures logs or network contents.

No account identifier, name, or email address is sent to either service.
Email addresses and token-like strings are redacted from captured events.
Telemetry is never used for advertising; there are no advertising SDKs and we
do not sell data.

## 2. What we don't do

- No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no data sales.
- No persistent storage of board images.

## 3. Data retention and your choices

Diagnostics and analytics are retained per Sentry's and PostHog's standard
retention policies. To request deletion of your account data (Apple
sign-in entitlements and subscription records), email
[support@tiletactician.com](mailto:support@tiletactician.com).
