## How scoring works

TileTactician calculates each legal placement from the board, rack, and active
board type:

1. **Base tile values** — each letter contributes its standard point value.
2. **Letter premiums** — double- and triple-letter squares multiply the tile
   value before it is added.
3. **Word premiums** — double- and triple-word squares multiply the word total.
4. **Cross-words** — perpendicular words formed by the placement are scored and
   added.
5. **Bingo bonus** — using all seven rack tiles adds 35 points on Crossplay
   boards and 50 points on Scrabble boards.

A blank is a wildcard: it can represent a letter, but contributes zero tile
points. The result displays the assigned blank letter and the score breakdown
for the placement.

## Play ranking

The native solver orders placements by score descending, then by word, and
returns at most 100 unique results. The Results screen can sort that returned
set by **Score**, **Length**, or **A–Z**. A lower-scoring legal play may be
outside the 100-result cap and absent from the list.

Tap a result to preview its placement; tap it again to open the detailed view.

## Rack management

The rack bar lets you:

- add tiles with the `+` control;
- tap a tile to remove it; and
- long-press a tile to toggle it as a blank wildcard.

Enter the rack before analyzing. The solver uses the rack together with the
board and selected board type to generate legal placements.

## Coach mode

The **Coach** slider has three assistance levels:

| Level | What the results show |
|---|---|
| **Hint** | Highlights valid play locations without revealing the letters |
| **Coach** | Shows up to 10 placements without their scores |
| **Expert** | Shows the full returned result set (up to 100) with score information |

These levels change what the Results screen reveals, not the search itself.

## Blunder bar

After a second scan of the same game, the results screen can compare the newly
added tiles with the solver placements. The **Blunder Bar** reports the matched
play's efficiency against the best available score, the played word and score,
the best word and score, and any missed points.

Its labels are based on efficiency:

| Efficiency | Label |
|---|---|
| 95–100% | Excellent |
| 80–94% | Good |
| 60–79% | OK |
| 40–59% | Blunder |
| Below 40% | Miss |

When the best placement uses all seven tiles and the matched play is shorter,
the bar also reports that a bingo was available. If no prior board or matching
placement is available, the bar is hidden.

## Word definitions

Tap the `?` control on a result to open the **Define** modal. It looks up and
shows:

- the word's definition;
- its optional part of speech; and
- its optional phonetic spelling.

The modal does not show an example sentence. It can show a loading state, a
missing definition, a timeout, or an unavailable response, and it caches
successful lookups while the app is running.
