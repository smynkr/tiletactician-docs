## How scoring works

TileTactician calculates scores using standard Scrabble rules:

1. **Base tile values** — each letter carries its standard point value (A=1,
B=3, C=3, D=2, E=1, F=4, G=2, H=4, I=1, J=8, K=5, L=1, M=3, N=1, O=1,
P=3, Q=10, R=1, S=1, T=1, U=1, V=4, W=4, X=8, Y=4, Z=10).

2. **Letter premiums** — double-letter and triple-letter squares multiply the
tile value before summing.

3. **Word premiums** — double-word and triple-word squares multiply the entire
word score. Multiple word premiums stack multiplicatively.

4. **Cross-words** — any perpendicular words formed by the placement are scored
and added to the total.

5. **Bingo bonus** — playing all seven rack tiles in one turn adds 50 bonus
points.

## Play ranking

Results are sorted by total score, highest first. When two plays score
identically, the solver breaks ties by:

- Fewer tiles used (preserves rack flexibility)

- Alphabetical word order (deterministic output)

## Rack management

The rack bar shows your current tiles. You can:

- **Add tiles** — tap the `+` button and select letters

- **Remove tiles** — tap a tile in the rack to return it

- **Set blanks** — long-press a tile to toggle it as a blank wildcard

## Performance

The solver is optimized for real-time analysis:

| Board state | Typical time (Apple Silicon) |
|---|---|
| Empty board, 7 tiles | < 200 ms |
| Mid-game board, 7 tiles | < 500 ms |
| All-duplicate rack (e.g., 7×E) | < 50 ms |

Results stream incrementally — the best play appears before the full search
completes.

## Dictionary

TileTactician uses the official tournament word list. The dictionary is
embedded in the app and does not require a network connection.

## Coach mode

The **Coach slider** adjusts how much assistance TileTactician provides during
analysis. Slide from full analysis (every legal play shown) down to guided
hints (only the top play, or just a nudge toward the best area of the board).

| Level | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Full** | Every legal placement, ranked by score |
| **Guided** | Top 3 plays with score and position |
| **Hint** | The best play's board region highlighted, no word shown |
| **Off** | No analysis — use for practice or tournament prep |

Coach mode is useful for learning: start with hints, check your instinct
against the solver, then reveal the full analysis to see what you missed.

## Blunder bar

After you make a play (in Play mode or manually), the **Blunder Bar** shows how
your choice compares to the solver's top pick:

- **Green** — you played the optimal move (or within 2 points of it)

- **Yellow** — a reasonable play, but 3–10 points below optimal

- **Red** — a blunder: more than 10 points left on the table

The bar also shows the word you played, the optimal word, and the point
difference. Tap it to expand the full result list and see where your play ranks.

Use the Blunder Bar to identify patterns in your play — consistent reds on
bingo opportunities, for example, suggest studying seven-letter word stems.

## Word definitions

Tap any word in the results list to open the **Define** modal, which shows the
word's definition, part of speech, and an example sentence. Useful for learning
unfamiliar tournament words and verifying that an obscure play is legitimate.
