TileTactician (CrossPlayer Pro) is a Scrabble and NYT Crossplay board
analysis app: enter the board, enter your rack, and every legal play appears
ranked by score in real time. The solver streams results as it searches — the
top play lands within milliseconds on a mid-game board — and the full
tournament dictionary is embedded, so analysis works fully offline.
