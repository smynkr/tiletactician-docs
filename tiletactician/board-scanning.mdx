Import a board from a photo or screenshot using TileTactician's built-in scanner — no manual tile entry needed.

## How it works

TileTactician can read a Scrabble or Crossplay board from a photo or
screenshot. Tap the **Scan Board** button on the board screen, and the app
uses on-device image recognition to identify tiles, premium squares, and grid
alignment.

## Scanning from the app

1. Open the board screen.

2. Tap the **Scan** button (camera icon) in the toolbar.

3. Point your camera at a physical board, or choose a screenshot from your
photo library.

4. The scanner highlights detected tiles. Review and correct any misreads.

5. Tap **Import** to load the board into the editor.

## Scanning from the Share Extension

On iOS, you can share a board screenshot from any app directly to
TileTactician. See [Share Extension](/tiletactician/share-extension) for setup.

## Accuracy tips

- **Flat, well-lit surface** — shadows and glare cause misreads

- **Fill the frame** — the board should occupy most of the image

- **Straight angle** — avoid extreme perspective; the scanner corrects mild
skew but not heavy distortion

- **Physical boards** — matte tiles scan better than glossy ones

## Common misreads

| Misread | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| O ↔ empty | Round tile on a light square | Tap the cell and set the correct letter |
| I ↔ L ↔ 1 | Narrow tiles in some fonts | Tap to correct |
| S ↔ 5 | Low-resolution images | Retake at higher resolution |
| Blank ↔ O | Unmarked blank tile | Toggle the `?` blank marker on the cell |

## Live Board Overlay

For physical board play, the **Live Board Overlay** projects the scanned board
onto your camera view in real time. This lets you verify tile placement against
the digital board without looking back and forth. Enable it from the board
screen toolbar (overlay icon).
