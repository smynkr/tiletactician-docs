## How it works

TileTactician can read a Scrabble or Crossplay board from a photo or
screenshot. Tap **Scan Board** on the home screen, and the app uploads the
image through its authenticated server proxy to Google Cloud Vision's
`DOCUMENT_TEXT_DETECTION` service. The OCR pipeline uses the Vision result to
identify tiles, premium-square labels, and grid geometry; additional server-
proxied AI verification passes can check ambiguous tiles or rack reads.

This recognition is **not on-device**. Settings shows Vision AI and enhanced AI
as **Server-managed**, and says board images are processed securely and never
stored. Board scanning therefore requires a signed-in account.

Each logical board scan uses one Vision scan credit. Crossplay Pro grants 50
monthly credits; signed-out users cannot scan, and users without a balance can
buy packs from **Settings**. Pack credits do not expire. The balance is checked
before scanning and refreshed after the request settles.

## Scanning from the app

1. Sign in with Apple if the app asks for an account, and confirm that you have
   a scan credit in **Settings**.
2. On the home screen, tap **Scan Board**.
3. Point your camera at a physical board, or choose a screenshot from your
   photo library.
4. Follow the progress labels as the pipeline finds the board, reads tiles,
   sharpens crops, fills gaps, and validates the result.
5. Review and correct uncertain reads, then confirm the board to load it into
   the editor.

The client sends a shared scan request ID with the Vision operations in one
logical scan, so rescue and verification calls are accounted for together. If a
scan is cancelled or fails, the server-side credit flow can settle or refund the
request according to its authoritative result.

## Scanning from the Share Extension

On iOS, you can share a board screenshot from any app directly to
TileTactician. See [Share Extension](/tiletactician/share-extension) for setup.
The same authenticated, credit-gated OCR pipeline handles the shared image.

## Accuracy tips

- **Flat, well-lit surface** — shadows and glare cause misreads.
- **Fill the frame** — the board should occupy most of the image.
- **Straight angle** — avoid extreme perspective; the scanner corrects mild
  skew but not heavy distortion.
- **Physical boards** — matte tiles scan better than glossy ones.
- **Screenshots** — crop tightly to the game board when possible, and avoid
  overlays or keyboards covering cells.

## Common misreads

| Misread | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| O ↔ empty | Round tile on a light square | Tap the cell and set the correct letter |
| I ↔ L ↔ 1 | Narrow tiles in some fonts | Tap to correct |
| S ↔ 5 | Low-resolution images | Retake at higher resolution |
| Blank ↔ O | Unmarked blank tile | Toggle the `?` blank marker on the cell |

## Scan reference overlay

After a scan, the **Show Scan** control in the editor toolbar overlays the
original scan image on top of the board grid, so you can verify recognized tiles
against the photo before analyzing. Toggle it off with **Hide Scan** to see the
board cleanly.
