---
title: "Privacy"
description: "TileTactician privacy policy."
---

TileTactician collects:

- **Board photos** you submit for scanning — processed for recognition and
  not stored.
- **Crash and usage diagnostics.**
- **Subscription entitlements** needed to honor your plan.

Payments are handled by Apple. We do not sell data.

For data-deletion requests, email
[support@tiletactician.com](mailto:support@tiletactician.com).
